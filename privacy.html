Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Who we are

Floating Boot is a small independent apparel brand. This policy covers floatingboot.com and the Floating Boot Instagram and Facebook accounts.

What this website collects

floatingboot.com has no accounts, forms or checkout. It does not ask you for any personal information. To understand visits and measure our ads, the site uses:

  • Google Analytics: anonymous visit statistics (pages viewed, device, approximate country).
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok Pixel: measure visits that come from our ads and help show our ads to people likely to be interested.

These services may set cookies and process your IP address and browser information under their own privacy policies: Google, Meta, TikTok. You can block them with your browser's cookie and tracking settings or an ad blocker.

Orders

All purchases happen on our store hosted by Fourthwall (floatingboot-shop.fourthwall.com). Your name, address, email and payment details are collected and processed by Fourthwall and its payment providers to fulfil your order, under Fourthwall's privacy policy. We only see the order details needed to handle it.

Social media

We publish posts and ads on Instagram, Facebook and TikTok using the platforms' official tools. We do not collect data from your social profiles beyond what the platforms show us as the account owner (for example comments and messages you send us).

Your rights and contact

We do not sell personal data. To ask a question or request that we delete any data we hold about you (for example a message you sent us), write to us via Instagram direct message at @floating_boot. For order data, you can also contact Fourthwall support.
