Lo Stivale — The Boot

Every tee starts with a place.

Milan's Duomo, the Colosseo, the light on Lake Como — the same landmarks that filled 1950s Italian travel posters. We redraw them in vintage-lithograph linework and in flat, minimal outline, city by city, the way a good travel poster always did.

Ukiyo-e — The Technique

Then we fold in a wave.

One edition per landmark borrows the woodblock line of Japanese ukiyo-e — the swell of a Hokusai wave, flattened perspective, bold negative space — and wraps it around the same Italian skyline. Same subject, one extra technique.
